What is this for?

This study is part of a research paper on QuantaColony, software for measuring microbial colonies in photos of plates. The paper asks how closely measurements agree when different people measure the same plates.

Your measurements, the time each step took and your answers on the first page are sent to Alejandro Navarro (University of California, Riverside). Your name only tells participants apart and does not appear in the paper.

The study takes about 15 minutes and runs entirely in this browser tab.
